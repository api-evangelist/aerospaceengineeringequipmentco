# Aerospace Engineering Equipment (AEE)

> Aerospace Engineering Equipment (Suzhou) Co., Ltd., trading as AEE, is a Chinese industrial equipment
> manufacturer founded in December 2011 that designs, builds and integrates friction stir welding (FSW)
> machines, automatic FSW production lines and FSW tooling. It is a wholly owned subsidiary of Shanghai
> Aerospace Equipment Manufacturing Factory (149 Factory) under the Eighth Academy of the China Aerospace
> Science and Technology Corporation (CASC), with an R&D headquarters in Suzhou and a production base in
> Liyang. It supplies aerospace and aviation, rail transit, marine and shipbuilding, new-energy automotive,
> nuclear and power-electronics manufacturers.

## What is and is not an API here

AEE publishes no developer program, no API documentation, no SDK and no machine-readable specification.
It sells capital welding machinery, not software. What exists is the WordPress core REST API behind its
English-language marketing site at a-fsw.com, which answers anonymous read requests. API Evangelist
documented that surface from the server's own route index and OPTIONS schema documents. Treat it as a
content and product-catalogue source, not as a supported product with an availability commitment.

## How to call it

- Base URL: `https://a-fsw.com/wp-json`
- Authentication: none. Every documented operation is an anonymous GET.
- Pagination: `page` and `per_page` (1-100; 999 returns HTTP 400 `rest_out_of_bounds`). Read `X-WP-Total`, `X-WP-TotalPages` and the RFC 8288 `Link` header.
- Trim responses with `_fields=id,slug,title,link`. Inline featured media and taxonomy terms with `_embed`.
- Filter products by category: `/wp-json/wp/v2/product?product-category=3` (3 = FSW Machine).
- Errors: WordPress `{code, message, data:{status}}` at `application/json` — NOT RFC 9457.
- KNOWN QUIRK: a miss on a single-item route (`/wp-json/wp/v2/product/999999`) is intercepted by the edge and returns an **nginx HTML 404**, not JSON. Branch on content-type before parsing.
- No rate-limit headers are published or observable. No `ETag` or `Last-Modified`, so conditional GET is unavailable.
